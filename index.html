The Legacy of Ceylon Cinnamon

Sri Lanka, formerly known as Ceylon, is the birthplace of true cinnamon. For centuries, our island nation has been renowned for producing the world's most exquisite cinnamon, prized by royalty and gourmet chefs alike. Ceylon Cinnamon (Cinnamomum verum) is distinctly different from common cassia, offering a delicate, sweet flavor with subtle citrus notes.

At Ridma Collection, we continue this proud tradition. Founded by K.G.P. Wasantha, our business is built on a foundation of integrity, quality, and passion for Sri Lanka's golden spice. We work directly with local farmers and processors to ensure every batch meets the highest international standards.
